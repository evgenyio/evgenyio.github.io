I’m Evgeny Nikiforov, a full-stack product engineer. These days I’m at Poolside, working on AI coding tools. Before that I led and built cool products at Dubformer, an AI dubbing startup, worked on the GKE console at Google Cloud, and did a lot of fun stuff at Yandex Translate. I get plenty done and enjoy it, finish what I start and care about the details. And I expect the same from the people around me.
