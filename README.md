# evgeny.io

The personal site of Evgeny Nikiforov, product engineer. Plain HTML, CSS and one script, no framework, no build step; served by GitHub Pages from this branch.

- `index.html`, `style.css`: the page, set like a book in Piazzolla: one serif, one ink, one left edge, no greys. The work history is drawn as dimension lines on one scale from 2012 to now; the small inline script at the end of `index.html` places them, shows the months on hover, and keeps the job you are reading on the scale, which stays pinned while the jobs scroll past: its years get a wash of the wet ink, and a click on the scale goes to any year.
- `script.js`: the two things that move at the top. A year counter that computes itself from January 2012 and rolls once on load, and the sea behind the headline: a WebGL canvas of small figures lying flat on the water, seen in perspective, the water simplex noise drifting towards you. Every visit opens on another stretch of water in another figure, squares, circles, crosses, pluses or triangles (`?t=<seconds>` picks the moment, `?fig=square` and so on the figure, `?fig=mix` lays them out in patches). The figures rock with the waves, tipping with the slope and closing to a line on a wave's back; moving the pointer barely stirs the water, and a click drops a ring in the next ink. Paused off-screen, one still frame under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Its look is set by the `--sea-*` custom properties on `.sea` and `.hero .sea` in `style.css`.
- `sea-foot.js`: the strip of sea at the foot of the page, under the contacts, drawn as a map legend: a dot in a trough, a circle on a slope, a triangle on a crest, so the waves read through the symbols. Same water, same pointer and clicks, its own canvas; its look is the `--sea-*` properties on `.sea`.
- `fonts/`: Piazzolla by Huerta Tipográfica, roman and italic, subset to Latin, with its licence (SIL OFL 1.1).
- `favicon.svg`, `favicon-32.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`: the sea as an icon; `favicon-gen.js` regenerates the SVG (`node favicon-gen.js`).
- `games/index.html`, `games/games.css`: the games page, one more chapter of the same book: a short entry for each game, with where to play it, where its code is, and four of its screens set as plates. The screens live in `assets/games/`.
- `og.png`, `assets/games/og.png`: the cards a link to the site and to the games page unfold into, 1200 by 630. Each is a screenshot of its own page, the front page's the header and the headline over the sea; `og-gen.js` retakes both with headless Chrome (`node og-gen.js`). Worth rerunning when a headline or the games change, and in January, when the counter turns over (the scale adds the new year by itself).
- `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`: everything a crawler is told. The sitemap lists the page, the games page and the apps under `projects/` and `games/`.
- `projects/` and `games/`: side projects deployed here by their own repositories, each into its own folder; not part of the site's sources, apart from the games page itself, which the deploys leave alone.

Project hover previews and asset provenance are documented in [docs/project-previews.md](docs/project-previews.md). Run a local static server and open `/#projects` or `/#games` to check the previews.
